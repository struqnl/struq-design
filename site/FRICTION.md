# FRICTION.md: where the Struq skill tripped this build

1. **typography.md §3.1 House pairing note vs §2.4 role tokens.** The note says Comfortaa sets the wordmark and h1 only, but the token contract has one `font-display` and one `text-display` (with one weight) for "the hero h1 and the footer ask". With the house pairing those two display lines are two faces with different rest weights (Comfortaa 400, Urbanist 300). I added `--font-brand` and set the ask with `font-light`. Fix: give the House pairing a row (role faces, display weight per face, a `--font-brand` token) like S1 to S7.

2. **vibe-presets.md Soft Modern hero entrance (weight-in) vs a face whose wght axis starts at 300.** typography.md §5 says "start from their minimum", which for Comfortaa is 300 to 400: barely visible. I declared a different entrance ("stack-in": the mark's bars drop in, plus the rise and the 300 to 400 weight). Fix: say in weight-in what to do when the face's minimum is within 100 of the rest weight (switch entrance, or rest at 400 and start at 300 only with a second gesture).

3. **composition.md §5 footer-reveal (fixed form).** The recipe needs the footer's height for `main`'s margin, and a JS-measured height means flipping the footer from flow to fixed after hydration: CLS 0.98 at 1440, and the fixed footer became the LCP element. A footer taller than the viewport also loses its top. The sticky form (`footer { position: sticky; bottom: 0 }` under a `relative z-10` main) needs no measuring and no shift. Fix: ship the sticky form as the default, and warn that a revealed footer is painted at load (keep it out of paint until near, or it can win LCP).

4. **pitfalls.md §3.6 / SKILL.md step 8, the empty-region check vs native lazy images.** A Playwright `fullPage` capture never scrolls, so every `next/image` below the fold (lazy by default) rendered as an empty frame in the reduced-motion capture. That is a capture artifact, not a page bug, but it reads exactly like the failure the check hunts. Fix: tell the check to walk the page with the wheel (works with JS off too) before a full-page capture, or to run it with `loading` forced eager.

5. **brand.md §3 wordmark script.** With opentype.js 1.3 and the Fontsource Comfortaa 600 WOFF, `getKerningValue` returned `undefined` (x became NaN for every glyph after the first) and `toPathData(2)` printed `NaN` control points for some shifted glyphs. The logo rendered as "S" and a dot. Fix: `|| 0` on the kern, and outline at the origin then shift, with a small own serializer (scripts/wordmark.mjs here has the working version). Also: the script has no hook for a brand's coloured full stop; I appended it as its own path.

6. **performance.md §1 budget with Radix in the header.** The mobile menu imported at the root put Radix Dialog in the first-load bundle (256 kB gzip, over 250). The skill names "Radix imported into the root layout" as a cut, but nav-header.md §3b's ship-this menu is exactly that shape. Fix: show the lazy-on-first-tap launcher (next/dynamic, open on mount) in nav-header.md §3b.

7. **line-rise-hero and LCP.** The CSS pre-split guard hides the h1 until hydration, and the recipes also hide the eyebrow, lead and CTA behind JS entrances, so throttled LCP was 2.7s on mobile. Moving the hero's non-split copy to a CSS keyframe brought it to 2.2s. Fix: say in page-entrances or typography that only the split line waits for JS; everything else in the hero enters in CSS.

8. **setup.md §0 Known API shifts.** Not listed: the Next 16.4 scaffold wires Tailwind through `@tailwindcss/turbopack` in `next.config.ts` (no postcss config), enables `partialPrefetching`, and writes `AGENTS.md` with em dashes that `next dev` re-adds. Fix: add rows, and a note on what to do with the scaffold's AGENTS.md.

9. **README/skill proof copy is a fact source the skill does not route to.** The brief said "summarize from SKILL.md and README"; the README's per-brief "Without / With" lines were the honest captions for the proof rows. direction.md MATERIALS could name "the product's own README or docs" as a materials source for software launches.
