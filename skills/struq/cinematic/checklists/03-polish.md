# Direction and Polish Checklist

- [ ] Each chapter has one dominant visual idea.
- [ ] Motion density varies across the experience.
- [ ] Every major movement has anticipation or consequence.
- [ ] Stable holds provide reading and comprehension time.
- [ ] Camera stops and copy land with intentional microtiming.
- [ ] The principal subject changes role across chapters.
- [ ] Bridge elements become visible before the boundary.
- [ ] Lighting begins preparing incoming states early.
- [ ] SVG and text motion share the visual axis.
- [ ] Pointer influence is not uniform across all chapters.
- [ ] Velocity effects have controlled attack and decay.
- [ ] Post-processing supports beats rather than masking weak composition.
- [ ] Mobile has authored shots and cover conditions.
- [ ] Reduced motion retains hierarchy and continuity.
- [ ] Slow-motion recordings reveal no jumps or accidental simultaneous landings.
